# Source granularity and quote-entry effort

- Context: owner corrected E01 gate recommendations for manual quote distances and address-level historical matching, 2026-10-26.
- Cause: those proposals did not fit quoting during a phone call or the available town-only invoice notes. Automatically matching words had also produced false route pairs, per the owner’s sanitised evidence account.
- Resolution: owner D35/D36 choose editable routed distance/country estimates and directed town identity with person-confirmed aliases. Full entered addresses remain routing inputs; unmatched invoice places remain visible. Planner distances do not prove measured kilometre completeness.
- Verification: both E01 design drafts trace those choices to D35/D36 and include negative cases for false/reversed/ambiguous town matches and unverified distances. Source coverage, live routing and dispatcher adoption were not tested by this documentation run.
- Prevention finding: evaluate an identity/matching proposal against source granularity and evaluate required manual entry against the actual decision moment. This is supporting knowledge, not a new standing rule or evidence that the product is adopted.
