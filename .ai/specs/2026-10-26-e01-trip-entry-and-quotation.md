# E01 — Trip entry, quotation and the truck’s next move

Status: **draft skeleton — blocked at the interactive Open Questions gate; not approved for implementation**.
Date: 2026-10-26. Subject: #18; specification milestone: #22; stories: #22–#26; invoice-history input: #28; later mockup: #21.

## 📝 TLDR

The dispatcher currently quotes from a single kilometre rate and plans the next load around each truck’s unloading. The proposed E01 capability records trips, prices one offer with its whole empty approach, mandatory wash answer and exact dates, and shows the single-trip result, next availability and similar historical trips. Design reusable foundations against the visible backlog, but implement only E01 consumers; first specify all E01, then check its synthetic-data mockup, then build the smallest clickable trip-entry-and-quote phase (D29).

## Open Questions

All options and recommendations below are proposals awaiting the owner. No answer is selected. Questions concern business consequences; technical contracts and detailed design follow the answers.

- **Q1 — Document granularity:** keep the whole E01 and its reusable-foundation design in one specification, or add a companion foundation specification? Recommendation: one E01 specification with a distinct foundations section; shared foundations currently have no independently deployable product consumer. A companion, if chosen, must not fragment E01 or create a framework-first delivery phase (D19, D29).
- **Q2 — Commercial unit and names:** for E01, does one accepted offer/order correspond to one truck trip, or must one order already support several trips/trucks? Recommendation: one-to-one for E01; “kurs” for the movement, “zlecenie” for the commercial reference, “wycena” before acceptance; defer bulk/multi-trip entry (brief Q02, E11a).
- **Q3 — First trip:** before a truck has a preceding entered unloading, require a dispatcher-entered starting place and time, or require an earlier trip first? Recommendation: explicit initial place/time and approach evidence for the first quote only; never invent availability for a truck with no trips (#23). Unknown initial approach blocks a complete quote.
- **Q4 — Kilometres and reconciliation evidence:** use manually entered approach/loaded distances with start/end odometer readings, or route-provider distances with manual confirmation? Recommendation: manual entry and odometer boundaries first, allowing “not verified” when readings are absent; route estimates must not be labelled proof of actual kilometre completeness (R05, R08, #24).
- **Q5 — Place identity:** does “same loading/unloading place” mean the same town, or the same loading site/address? Recommendation: stable site/address identities with confirmed spelling aliases; no fuzzy automatic merging of invoice routes. Unresolved invoice places remain unmatched (D28, #28).
- **Q6 — Cost inputs and missing sources:** for the first quote, use owner-maintained dated consumption/fuel/toll/AdBlue/exchange-rate assumptions, or require external providers before quoting? Recommendation: dated manual assumptions per vehicle/country as needed, marked estimated, without per-driver estimates (brief Q04 remains open); future-date fallback uses latest prior known values with date/reason, and missing required inputs block a complete result. A05 is not validated by this choice.
- **Q7 — Currency and price basis:** quote and compare net amounts in EUR with dated conversion of other source currencies, or support several quote currencies from the first phase? Recommendation: EUR net for E01, retain original source currency and rate/date; never compare net with gross or invent missing currency. This is a proposal, not an adopted legacy default.
- **Q8 — Precision and rounding:** approve a concrete decimal policy now, or provide another policy before calculations are designed? Recommended proposal: money totals 2 decimals, unit prices/exchange rates/consumption 6, distance and litres 3; Decimal arithmetic precision 40 significant digits with validated input bounds, half-up ties, no intermediate component rounding, final totals rounded once and displayed components reconciled explicitly; verdicts compare the same rounded totals the user sees. PostgreSQL precision/ranges and synthetic tie/aggregation examples require confirmation during design (R03).
- **Q9 — Time and fixed costs:** allocate equipment/labour costs over actual elapsed time from previous unloading (or initial start) through this unloading, including waiting, wash and rest, or retain the older rounded half-day/minimum-day model? Recommendation: elapsed time with separate owner-maintained equipment/labour rates and an explicit weekend labour rule; use each site’s local timezone for entry and display, retaining unambiguous instants. No hidden half-day/minimum charges (R07).
- **Q10 — Profit, overhead and recommendation:** show revenue minus full costs plus a target price solved for an owner-set markup after overhead, or show the result without an accept/negotiate recommendation until that policy is agreed? Recommendation: show full costs, overhead and final result separately, with no automatic verdict initially. If a target is wanted, owner must specify overhead basis/rate, markup versus margin, and target rate; a price derived from an existing offer is not a solved minimum price (older review §8).
- **Q11 — Wash detours and charges:** include declared wash/refuelling detour kilometres and time in the trip containing the stop, or permit only direct-route calculations initially? Recommendation: include declared stops with their dates, distance and country breakdown, charging each kilometre once. Preserve R06: ours is cost, client-paid amount is revenue; confirm whether a client-paid wash can also incur a separate carrier expense, rather than inventing an expense or netting it silently.
- **Q12 — Acceptance and corrections:** does “accept” freeze the shown input/rate/calculation version, with explicit cancellation/correction and successor warnings, or may edits replace an accepted quote? Recommendation: freeze on acceptance; corrections create revisions and invalidate affected unaccepted successor quotes without rewriting accepted history. Decide whether accepting a financially negative offer requires a reason or is prohibited; recommendation: allow with a recorded reason, since D22 defers chain judgement.
- **Q13 — Open approach at a period boundary:** keep empty travel after the final unloading visible as unassigned across months until the next trip, or require a separate terminal-movement workflow now? Recommendation: keep it open, retain its evidence/date, and never charge it to the preceding trip; unknown distance stays unknown, not zero. Vehicle withdrawal remains visible as unresolved rather than closing kilometres by invention (R08).
- **Q14 — Invoice-history access:** read a limited, read-only accounting API/view, or import a controlled export first? Recommendation: a limited read-only interface if available; otherwise a controlled export using the same boundary. Confirm which is available without sharing credentials or records. Import dates/prices/currencies/source IDs and directed place pairs only as needed, deduplicate revisions and expose mapping failures; no inferred result or empty kilometres (D28, #28).
- **Q15 — Access for the first running phase:** named dispatcher and owner accounts with owner-managed membership in one company, or an existing company identity provider? Recommendation: named accounts using a maintained authentication solution and minimal dispatcher/owner permissions; confirm whether a company identity provider exists. No public real-data pilot, shared login or client-selected company scope; implementation choice follows requirements, not a custom authentication framework.

## 📝 Proposed Solution — Foundation Design Agenda

This is a backlog-derived inventory for design **after the gate**, not selected interfaces, entities or an implementation plan. No domain implementation exists and no approved E01 specification was found.

Existing foundations to reuse: `makeCrudRoute` for CRUD, `apiHandler` for business operations, Zod/decimal-string/application-error contracts, Awilix request scopes and isolated EntityManager, `apiCall`, `CrudForm`, `DataTable`, feedback and translated Polish messages. Authentication callbacks have no production identity implementation, and ORM scope is not tenant authorization. Forms currently support flat string fields, not all quote interactions; extensions must compose the shared components rather than fork them.

Backlog feature families and implications to investigate:

- Manual records and chronological schedules (#22–#24; later bulk entry in #20): scoped repositories, explicit operations, date/place/asset references, conflict detection; shared forms, date/decimal/place inputs and list patterns. Do not create a universal repository or service hierarchy.
- Financial decisions (#25; later component edits and settlement in #20): pure calculations with explicit policy/rates/time inputs, exact values/units/currencies, immutable accepted revisions and transaction/retry semantics; reusable value-with-source presentation and result breakdown.
- Historical evidence (#26/#28; later GPS/cards/invoices/email in #20): narrow provider interfaces, normalized evidence/provenance, source identity/revision, deduplication and unresolved mappings; reusable source, certainty, missing/conflicting and import-status presentation. Build only the invoice-history reader E01 requires, not general ingestion infrastructure or E4.
- Read projections (#23/#24/#26; later plan-versus-actual/monthly/customer/route reports in #20): reuse approved trip boundaries and calculation outputs, preserve phase/certainty/coverage, avoid duplicate formulas; controlled tables and purpose-specific comparisons. Do not implement future reports now.
- Discovery/evidence tasks (#19, #29–#31): prerequisites for later requirements, not justification to build email extraction, management reports or accounting reconciliation in E01. Q04/Q08/Q12/Q17/Q18 remain open in the brief and are not silently answered here.
- Later monitoring, during-trip control and forwarding (#20): retain explicit ownership and provider boundaries; no event bus, plugin loader, microservices or unused modules without an E01 consumer.

Post-gate design must identify each primitive’s E01 consumer and future backlog consumers, owner and package boundary, existing versus new status, minimal first implementation and deferred extensions. Pure core stays independent of DB/framework/DI; orchestration lives in app/server, persistence in db, generic UI in ui. Security, scope, versions and exact values are constraints, not optional future upgrades.

Acceptance/validation agenda after answers: trace all story criteria and active rules to contracts and synthetic cases; obtain approved financial examples; verify two-tenant SQL isolation, concurrency/retries, decimal round trips, missing sources, date boundaries and neighbouring-trip edits; require 100% unit coverage plus browser integration screenshots for changed flows. Detailed architecture, data model/graph, API contracts, UI states, aligned phase/step plans, external research and independent review are intentionally not authored before the interactive gate. D29 fixes the first build outcome to trip entry and quotation; no foundation-only delivery precedes it.
