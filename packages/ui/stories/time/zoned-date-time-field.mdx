# Zoned date-time field

`import { ZonedDateTimeField } from "@fleet-platform/ui/components/time/zoned-date-time-field"` · `import { resolveLocalDateTime, toInstant } from "@fleet-platform/ui/lib/zoned-time"`

## 1. Purpose

Lets the dispatcher type a date and time as the client says it ("12.11.2026", "09:30"), in the place's zone, and turns it into an exact instant with its zone and offset (P07, TIME-1). A time that does not exist or exists twice because of a clock change is never guessed.

## 2. When to use

- Schedule anchors of a trip: loading and end of unloading, each with its timezone and offset (E01 `TripDraftInput`).
- The vehicle's starting point: when the truck finishes its current work (s08).
- The wash date for "Nasze mycie", with `timeOptional`: date mandatory, time optional (D63).

## 3. When not to use

| Situation | Use instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Showing a time | Text formatted by the application in the place's zone | Entry and display are separate |
| A month or period (reports, closing) | A month picker, not yet in the system | E7 work; goes to "What is missing" |
| Picking a date from a calendar | Nothing yet; may come later (owner decision 2026-10-29) | Typed text keeps the format independent of the browser |
| A date in a flat reference form | Nothing yet: `CrudForm` has text, e-mail and textarea fields | The foundations keep `CrudForm`'s field API unchanged; a need goes to "What is missing" |

## 4. Anatomy

Label, hint and error from `Field`; a group with the date box ("DD.MM.RRRR") and the time box ("GG:MM", 24 hours), each showing its format while empty; the zone's name after them, also read as part of the description. When the time occurs twice, a required choice of the occurrence appears under the group.

## 5. Variants

| Variant | Result | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Date and time (default) | Instant, zone and offset | Loading, end of unloading, vehicle start |
| `timeOptional` | Instant when a time is given; otherwise the local date alone | Wash date (D63) |

The zone comes from the place (`timeZone`, IANA); the field does not let the dispatcher change it.

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility

- Two text boxes, not the browser's date and time inputs, whose format follows the browser's language ("10/31/2027", "02:30 AM"); no calendar for now (owner decision 2026-10-29). The date is "DD.MM.RRRR" and the time "GG:MM" in 24 hours; a one-digit day, month or hour is accepted ("2.11.2026", "9:30"). Dates that do not exist ("31.02.2027") are rejected.
- When focus leaves the whole field, it calls `onZonedChange` with the result: `{ ok: true, date, time, timeZone, offset, instant }`, or a reason: `empty`, `missingDate`, `missingTime`, `invalidDate`, `invalidTime`, `nonexistent`, `ambiguous` (with both offsets). `date` and `time` in the result are ISO ("2026-11-12", "09:30"). Moving between its two boxes checks nothing; nothing is checked while typing (owner decision 2026-10-29).
- A repeated time shows the choice of occurrence; choosing reports the resolved value at once. Changing the date or time removes the choice until the field is checked again.
- The group is named by the `Field` label; each box is named by the label and its own word ("Załadunek Data", "Załadunek Godzina"); hint, error and zone are in `aria-describedby`; `aria-invalid` and `required` come from `Field`.

## 8. Content

- The zone is named in words the dispatcher uses: "Czas warszawski".
- Every message says what to do: "Wpisz godzinę załadunku.", "Nie rozpoznano daty. Wpisz ją jako dzień.miesiąc.rok, np. 12.11.2026."
- A skipped time says why and what to enter: "Tej godziny nie ma: tej nocy zegar przesuwa się do przodu. Wpisz godzinę sprzed zmiany albo po niej."
- The choice of occurrence asks one question: "Ta godzina wystąpi tej nocy dwa razy. Którą wybierasz?", with "02:30 przed cofnięciem zegara" and "02:30 po cofnięciu zegara".

## 10. API

`ZonedDateTimeField`: `timeZone` (IANA, required), `zoneLabel`, `labels` (`date`, `dateFormat`, `time`, `timeFormat`, `occurrence`, `earlier`, `later`), `timeOptional`, `defaultDate` (ISO, shown as "12.11.2026"), `defaultTime`, `disabled`, `onZonedChange(result)`. `resolveLocalDateTime(date, time, timeZone)` → `unique` with the offset, `ambiguous` with both offsets (earlier instant first) or `nonexistent`; `toInstant(date, time, offset)` → ISO instant in UTC; `parseLocalDate("12.11.2026")` → "2026-11-12" or null, `parseLocalTime("9:30")` → "09:30" or null, `formatLocalDate`. All use `Intl` and `Date` only.

## 11. Code

```tsx
const [result, setResult] = useState<ZonedDateTimeResult | null>(null);

<Field label="Załadunek" hint="Dokładna data i godzina u klienta" error={result && !result.ok ? t(`time.${result.reason}`) : undefined} required>
  <ZonedDateTimeField timeZone={place.timeZone} zoneLabel={t(`zone.${place.timeZone}`)} labels={zonedDateTimeLabels} onZonedChange={setResult} />
</Field>
// send { loadingAt: result.instant, timeZone: result.timeZone, offset: result.offset } only when result.ok
```

## 12. Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Send the instant with the place's zone and offset. | Send "2027-10-31 02:30" and let the server guess. |
| Ask which 02:30 when the clock goes back. | Pick the first occurrence silently. |
| Reject 02:30 on the night the clock goes forward. | Move it to 03:30 without telling the dispatcher. |
