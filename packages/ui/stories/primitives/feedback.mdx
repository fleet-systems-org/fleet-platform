# Feedback messages

`import { LoadingMessage } from "@fleet-platform/ui/backend/feedback/LoadingMessage"` · `SuccessMessage` · `ErrorMessage` · `EmptyState` (same folder)

## 1. Purpose

Tells the dispatcher, in one sentence where the content would be, whether something is loading, worked, failed or is empty, and what to do next.

## 2. When to use

- `LoadingMessage`: while a list or a quote loads ("Liczenie wyceny…").
- `SuccessMessage`: after a saved action, with what changed ("Kurs przyjęty. Zestaw 01 będzie wolny…").
- `ErrorMessage`: a form-level or list-level failure (`CrudForm`, `DataTable`).
- `EmptyState`: a list with no rows, with the reason and one action when there is one (P9, R16.1).

## 3. When not to use

| Situation | Use instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A field is wrong | The `error` of its `Field` | Next to the control (WCAG 3.3.1) |
| A whole result cannot be used yet and needs a title and an action | Alert | More than one sentence |
| A notification that disappears | Not in the system (Q5c: no toast) | Messages stay where they happened |
| A history source is unavailable | `EmptyState` with a distinct sentence | "No history", "source unavailable" and "unmatched places" are shown differently and never block the quote (spec: History) |

## 4. Anatomy

`LoadingMessage`, `SuccessMessage`: one paragraph. `ErrorMessage`: `IconAlertCircle` (required) and text. `EmptyState`: text (required) and one action (optional, a Button).

## 5. Variants

None; the four messages are the variants.

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility

- Live regions announce a message that replaces content after an action: `status` politely, `alert` at once.
- Messages are not focusable; after a failed submit focus goes to the first invalid field (`CrudForm`).
- The error icon is `aria-hidden`; the words carry the meaning (WCAG 1.4.1). The `EmptyState` action is at least 36 × 36 px (WCAG 2.5.8).

## 8. Content

- Every message says what happened and what to do (checklist item 12): "Nie udało się zapisać kursu. Wpisane dane są w formularzu; sprawdź połączenie i zapisz ponownie."
- Loading names the work, with an ellipsis: "Wczytywanie floty…".
- Empty says why it is empty, not only that it is: "Ten zestaw nie ma jeszcze kursów."
- At most 65 characters per line (`max-w-measure`, audit finding 15).

## 9. Responsive

- 1440 and 768 px: in place of the content they replace.
- 320 px: text wraps; the action goes under the text.

## 10. API

| Component | Props |
|---|---|
| `LoadingMessage`, `SuccessMessage` | `children`: the sentence |
| `ErrorMessage` | `children`: the sentence; `id`: to point at it with `aria-describedby` |
| `EmptyState` | `children`: the sentence; `action`: one optional element, usually a Button |

## 11. Code

```tsx
<EmptyState>Brak historii cen na tej trasie. To nie blokuje wyceny.</EmptyState>
```

```tsx
{loading ? <LoadingMessage>Liczenie wyceny…</LoadingMessage>
  : error ? <ErrorMessage>{error}</ErrorMessage>
  : trips.length === 0 ? <EmptyState action={<Button variant="secondary" onClick={newTrip}>Wpisz kurs</Button>}>Ten zestaw nie ma jeszcze kursów. Wpisz pierwszy kurs, żeby policzyć wycenę.</EmptyState>
  : <TripList trips={trips} />}
```

## 12. Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Nie udało się zapisać kursu. Wpisane dane są w formularzu…" | "Błąd 500". |
| "Ten zestaw nie ma jeszcze kursów. Wpisz pierwszy kurs…" | "Brak danych" on an empty list: it hides why. |
| Plain "Kurs przyjęty…" in default text. | A green toast that disappears before it is read (Q5c). |

## 13. Related
